🏴‍☠️ Pirate Seas

You are a pirate captain. Plunder fat merchant ships, outgun rival pirates, outrun the Navy when your Wanted level climbs, and lie low in pirate havens.

📖 How to play
🎓 New here? The guided Tutorial starts automatically on a new game (replay it any time).
🕹️ Steer: drag anywhere on the sea (a short drag slows down). Sails stay set when you let go. Prefer buttons? Turn on ◀ ▶ steering buttons in ☰ Captain's Log.
💥 Fire: LEFT / RIGHT buttons shoot broadsides out the sides of your ship.
⚓ Ports: sail close to a pier and tap Dock to repair, upgrade, hire crew, trade and take jobs.
🗺️ Tap the minimap for the big map. Waters get more dangerous far from Port Royal.
🎯 AUTO: the 🎯 button (or T) toggles auto-fire — your gunners aim and shoot hostile ships in range on their own. Broadsides hit hardest, so turn your side toward the enemy.
💰 Merchants: fire on them yourself to raid them (AUTO joins in only after you've attacked one). Badly damaged merchants may surrender — sail close and tap Board for extra loot.
🚨 Wanted: raiding raises your Wanted ★ level and the Navy hunts you. It fades if you lie low; pay it off at 🏴‍☠️ pirate havens. ⚖️ Lawful ports charge wanted captains more and turn away ★★★+ unless bribed.
🦈 Sharks circle the deeper waters (harmless near Port Royal). They gather where ships sink and bite when your hull is low — shoot them for 🦷 shark teeth. Beware the Megalodon far out at sea!
⛵ Fleet: seize surrendered merchants (⛓ Seize) or hire escorts at pirate havens. They follow and fight, but cost wages each minute. Manage them in port ➜ ⛵ Fleet.
📜 Jobs: treasure maps, smuggling, rescues, convoy raids, shark hunts, regattas, blockade runs and bounty contracts. Track the active one under your HUD (tap it to switch).
🎖️ Level up: earn XP to gain skill points (☰ ➜ Skills). Higher pirate ranks unlock bigger hulls at havens: Sloop ➜ Brig ➜ Frigate ➜ Galleon.
🔧 Your hull slowly repairs itself when no enemies are near.
🚀 BOOST: +25% speed for a few seconds, but the ship turns sluggishly. Then it recharges.
⌨️ Keyboard: WASD/arrows, Q/E fire, Space both, B or Shift boost, F dock, M map, T auto-fire.
